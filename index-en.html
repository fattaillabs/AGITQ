Features

Five minutes a day, strictly by the rules

This app does one simple thing — it computes the rules for you every day and speaks up only when the signal changes. The rest of the time, you can forget the market.

7 proven strategies

From the original 200-day SMA to QQQ 3/161, Hybrid, Score-based and Snowball — pick strategies refined by community sources and backtests.

Signal-change alerts

Notifications only when your position should change — golden/dead crosses, overheat entry and exit. Confirmation days (0/2/3) are configurable.

Morning market briefing

At 9 a.m., a summary of the signal state and market as of yesterday's close. One glance on your commute decides today's action.

Home-screen widget

Today's signal, market temperature and disparity live on your home screen. Custom ticker widgets are supported too.

Backtesting

Run your exact settings — strategy, confirmation days, envelope, profit-taking — on historical data, with TQQQ·SPYM·SGOV rotation included.

Profit milestones · Custom

Manage partial profit-taking at +10/+25/+50% and Snowball "graduation" with milestone alerts, and tune parameters your way.
