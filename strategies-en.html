Common rules

Common to all strategies: simple moving averages (SMA) on daily closes. Overheat is judged by a +5% envelope above the long-term line, and market temperature is split into Falling (below the line) / Focus (line to +5%) / Overheated (above +5%). On sell signals, funds rotate into an S&P ETF (default SPYM) or cash-like bonds (default SGOV). Signal-confirmation days (0/2/3) and partial profit-taking style are configurable in the app.

Backtest charts on this page reproduce the app’s rules on Yahoo Finance daily data (last 10 years, adjusted closes) for reference only.
