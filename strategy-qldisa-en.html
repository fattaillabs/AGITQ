How it works

Volatility and drawdowns are gentler, making it easier to hold through noise and well-suited to tax-deferred accounts (the Korean ISA in the original write-up). FAQ

Why QLD over TQQQ?
Lower expected return, but much shallower drawdowns. In accounts where losses can’t offset taxes, avoiding deep drawdowns helps after-tax results.
Do I need an ISA?
No — it works in any account as the “milder leverage” option.
Can I run this alongside a TQQQ strategy?
Yes — the source series suggests it. A common setup separates accounts: QLD in the tax-advantaged one, TQQQ 3/161 in a regular account.
How is this different from just holding QQQ?
Unlike buy-and-hold, it avoids falling regimes, so the time spent in the market is higher quality. Compounding 2x leverage while skipping drawdowns beat QQQ buy-and-hold on both return and MDD in backtests.
