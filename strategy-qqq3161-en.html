Signals from QQQ’s 3×161-day SMAs, trades in TQQQ — the combination found by the optimization series.

How it works

A community optimization series swept moving-average combinations exhaustively and found that taking signals from QQQ with 3/161 beats TQQQ-based signals by roughly 2%p per year with far fewer trades. When the 3-day SMA crosses above the 161-day, buy TQQQ; when it crosses below, exit fully into the rotation asset.

FAQ

Why signal on QQQ?
QQQ is less noisy than TQQQ, so even a short 3-day SMA whipsaws less. It separates signal stability from leveraged returns.
Is 161 a magic number?
It came from an exhaustive sweep. The series also showed n2 = 210–230 works — think of it as a band, not a magic constant.
